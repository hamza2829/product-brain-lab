# Personas

## Product manager

Owns a workflow and spends time recreating the context behind backlog items and customer requests. Often starts with a feature nickname or a fragment from a conversation. Needs to find when and why the decision happened, verify the wording against the original record, and carry a durable link into a planning note.

Example question — “Why did guided setup ask for a teammate before a CSV?”

## Chief product officer

Reviews bets across teams. Needs to recover the scope, customer evidence, and measurement plan before a portfolio or launch review. Wants to see the source and date of each claim, check who supplied it, and recognize when the available records are thin.

Example question — “Which measures are in the mobile approvals pilot, and where are they defined?”
